What does a mirror symmetry tell you about the eigenstates?

A particle in a symmetric double well. Apply the parity operation to the eigenstates, raise the barrier, then tilt the potential by a tiny amount and watch the states decide which well to live in.

Computed: finite differences, 100 points \(H = -\tfrac12\partial_x^2 + V_0\big((x/x_0)^2-1\big)^2 + \varepsilon x\), \(x_0 = 1.5\), \(\hbar = m = 1\), hard walls at \(x = \pm 3.2\)
Source: model knowledge. Standard treatment: Sakurai & Napolitano, Modern Quantum Mechanics, ch. 4 (symmetry in quantum mechanics); Landau & Lifshitz, Quantum Mechanics, ch. VII (the quasi-classical case).
