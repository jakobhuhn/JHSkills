Quantum mechanics · Perturbation theory

Does adding more orders of perturbation theory always help?

The ground energy of a two-level system has an exact formula and a perturbation series in the coupling \(\lambda\). Step up the order and watch where the series converges, and where it falls apart.

Exact formulas \(H = a\,\sigma_z + \lambda\,\sigma_x\), \(E_-(\lambda) = -\sqrt{a^2+\lambda^2}\); \(\hbar = 1\), energies in units of the coupling scale
Source: model knowledge. Standard treatment: Sakurai & Napolitano, Modern Quantum Mechanics, ch. 5; Bender & Orszag, Advanced Mathematical Methods for Scientists and Engineers, ch. 7–8.
