What does a single quantum measurement tell you?

Prepare a qubit, choose a measurement axis, and record shots one at a time or in batches. Watch the frequencies settle onto the Born probabilities, and how slowly they do it.

Exact probabilities, sampled outcomes \(\ket{\psi} = \cos\tfrac{\theta}{2}\ket{0} + e^{i\varphi}\sin\tfrac{\theta}{2}\ket{1}\); outcomes \(\pm1\) of \(\vec\sigma\cdot\hat n\); tilted axis \(\hat n = (\sin\alpha, 0, \cos\alpha)\); seeded random numbers
Source: model knowledge. Standard treatment: Nielsen & Chuang, Quantum Computation and Quantum Information, ch. 2; Sakurai & Napolitano, Modern Quantum Mechanics, ch. 1.
