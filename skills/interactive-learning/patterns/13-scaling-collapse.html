Critical phenomena · Finite-size scaling

How do curves for different system sizes become one?

A finite system cannot diverge: its susceptibility peak is rounded and shifted. Rescale both axes by the right powers of the size \(L\) and all the curves fall on top of each other.

Schematic: exact 2D Ising exponents, illustrative scaling function \(\chi_L(t) = L^{\gamma/\nu} f(t L^{1/\nu})\), \(\nu = 1\), \(\gamma = 7/4\), \(t = (T - T_c)/T_c\)
Source: model knowledge. Standard treatment: Cardy, Scaling and Renormalization in Statistical Physics, ch. 4; Newman & Barkema, Monte Carlo Methods in Statistical Physics, ch. 8.
