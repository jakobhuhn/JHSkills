Wave mechanics · Superposition

Why do more slits give sharper, brighter fringes?

Each path contributes a unit arrow in the complex plane, turned by \(\delta\) relative to the previous one. Add the arrows head to tail; the squared length of the sum is what the screen records.

Exact for equal-amplitude paths \(I(\delta) = \big|\sum_{j=0}^{N-1} e^{ij\delta}\big|^2\), unit amplitude per path, single-slit envelope ignored
Source: model knowledge. Standard treatment: Hecht, Optics, ch. 10 (diffraction, many slits); Feynman, Leighton & Sands, The Feynman Lectures on Physics Vol. I, ch. 30.
