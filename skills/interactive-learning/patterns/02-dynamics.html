Quantum optics · Driven two-level system

What does a resonant drive do to a two-level atom?

Switch on a drive of strength \(\Omega\) and detuning \(\Delta\), then play or scrub time. The left plot shows the populations, the right one the same state as a vector on the Bloch sphere.

Exact for this Hamiltonian Rotating frame, RWA: \(H = \tfrac{\Delta}{2}\sigma_z + \tfrac{\Omega}{2}\sigma_x\), \(\hbar = 1\), start in \(\ket{\downarrow}\); times in units of \(1/\Omega_0\)
Source: model knowledge. Standard treatment: Sakurai & Napolitano, Modern Quantum Mechanics, ch. 5 (spin magnetic resonance); Foot, Atomic Physics, ch. 7.
