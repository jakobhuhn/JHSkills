Quantum mechanics · Two-level systems

Why do energy levels refuse to cross?

Tune the detuning \(\varepsilon\) of two states and watch their energies approach. A coupling \(\Delta\) turns the crossing into an avoided crossing, and the two eigenstates swap character as you pass it.

\(H = \tfrac{\varepsilon}{2}\sigma_z + \Delta\,\sigma_x\) in the basis \(\ket{1}, \ket{2}\); energies in units of the coupling scale, \(\hbar = 1\)
Source: model knowledge. Standard treatment: Cohen-Tannoudji, Diu & Laloë, Quantum Mechanics vol. 1, ch. IV; Landau & Lifshitz, Quantum Mechanics, ch. XI (intersection of terms).
