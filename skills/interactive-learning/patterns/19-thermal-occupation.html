Where do twenty particles sit at temperature \(T\)?

Put the same twenty particles in a harmonic trap as fermions, bosons or classical particles. Drag the temperature and watch Pauli blocking, condensation and the classical limit appear on the level ladder.

3D isotropic harmonic trap, \(\hbar\omega = k_B = 1\), \(\varepsilon_n = n\), \(g_n = (n+1)(n+2)/2\), \(N = 20\) spinless particles, no interactions
Source: model knowledge. Standard treatment: Pethick & Smith, Bose–Einstein Condensation in Dilute Gases, ch. 2; Ashcroft & Mermin, Solid State Physics, ch. 2; Pathria & Beale, Statistical Mechanics, chs. 6–8.
