What does a mirror symmetry tell you about the eigenstates?

A particle in a symmetric double well. Mirror the two lowest eigenstates and each one comes back as plus or minus itself. Raise the barrier and the two energies squeeze together.

\(H = -\tfrac12\partial_x^2 + V_0\big((x/x_0)^2-1\big)^2\), \(x_0 = 1.5\), \(\hbar = m = 1\), hard walls at \(x = \pm 3.2\); finite differences, 100 points
Source: model knowledge. Standard treatment: Sakurai & Napolitano, Modern Quantum Mechanics, ch. 4 (symmetry in quantum mechanics); Landau & Lifshitz, Quantum Mechanics, ch. VII (the quasi-classical case).
