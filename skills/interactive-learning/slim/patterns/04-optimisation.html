How good is the best wavefunction you can guess?

Drag the trial point along the energy landscape \(E(\alpha)\) of a Gaussian guess for the hydrogen ground state. Every guess gives an energy above the true one; the best guess sits where the slope vanishes.

Hydrogen, atomic units: \(\hbar = m_e = e = 4\pi\varepsilon_0 = 1\), energies in hartree, \(E_0 = -1/2\)
Source: model knowledge. Standard treatment: Griffiths, Introduction to Quantum Mechanics (2nd ed.), ch. 7; Sakurai & Napolitano, Modern Quantum Mechanics, ch. 5.
