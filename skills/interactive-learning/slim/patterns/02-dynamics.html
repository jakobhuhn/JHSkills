Quantum optics · Driven two-level system

How long must a pulse last to flip an atom?

A resonant drive turns the state of a two-level atom at a steady rate. Switch it on for a time \(\tau\), then off, and see where the state ends up.

Rotating frame, RWA: \(H = \tfrac{\Delta}{2}\sigma_z + \tfrac{\Omega}{2}\sigma_x\), \(\hbar = 1\), start in \(\ket{\downarrow}\); times in units of \(1/\Omega\) (pulse mode: \(\Omega = 1\))
Source: model knowledge. Standard treatment: Sakurai & Napolitano, Modern Quantum Mechanics, ch. 5 (spin magnetic resonance); Foot, Atomic Physics, ch. 7.
