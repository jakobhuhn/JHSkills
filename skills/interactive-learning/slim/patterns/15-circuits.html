How does a circuit build entanglement one gate at a time?

Two qubits start in \(\ket{00}\). Step through the gates with Forward and Back and watch the four amplitudes and their phases change. Only some steps create entanglement.

Basis \(\ket{q_0 q_1}\); \(H = (X+Z)/\sqrt2\), \(S = \mathrm{diag}(1, \ii)\); CNOT flips the target when the control is 1
Source: model knowledge. Standard treatment: Nielsen & Chuang, Quantum Computation and Quantum Information, ch. 1, 2 and 4; Preskill, Lecture Notes for Physics 219, ch. 4.
