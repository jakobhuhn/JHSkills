Where does a system flow when you zoom out?

Each point is a pair of couplings: the stiffness of a 2D superfluid film and the fugacity of its vortices. Zooming out changes both. Drop a ball anywhere and watch where the flow carries it.

\(\dd x/\dd l = -y^2,\ \dd y/\dd l = -xy\); \(x = \pi K - 2\), \(y\) = vortex fugacity, \(l = \ln(\text{length scale})\); leading order, constants absorbed
Source: model knowledge. Standard treatment: Chaikin & Lubensky, Principles of Condensed Matter Physics, ch. 9; Kardar, Statistical Physics of Fields, ch. 8.
