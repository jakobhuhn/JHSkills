Statistical mechanics · Correlations in one dimension

How far does one spin know about another?

Pick a site on an Ising chain and see how strongly every other spin is correlated with it. Lower the temperature and watch the length over which spins agree.

\(H = -J\sum_i s_i s_{i+1}\), \(h = 0\), \(k_B = 1\), \(T\) in units of \(J\); open chain of \(N = 40\) sites
Source: model knowledge. Standard treatment: Chaikin & Lubensky, Principles of Condensed Matter Physics, ch. 3; Yeomans, Statistical Mechanics of Phase Transitions, ch. 5.
