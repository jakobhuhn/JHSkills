Topological matter · Winding number

When can a whole number change?

As \(k\) runs once around the Brillouin zone, the vector \(\mathbf d(k)\) traces a closed loop. Count how many times it winds around the origin, then try to change that count by deforming the loop.

\(\mathbf d(k) = (v + w\cos k + u\cos 2k,\; w\sin k + u\sin 2k)\), \(h(k) = \mathbf d(k)\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma\), \(k \in [0, 2\pi)\), lattice constant 1
Source: model knowledge. Standard treatment: Asbóth, Oroszlány & Pályi, A Short Course on Topological Insulators, ch. 1 (the SSH model); Bernevig & Hughes, Topological Insulators and Topological Superconductors.
