Quantum mechanics · Perturbation theory

Does adding more orders of perturbation theory always help?

The ground energy of a two-level system has an exact formula and a perturbation series in the coupling \(\lambda\). Raise the order and see where the series converges and where it falls apart.

\(H = a\,\sigma_z + \lambda\,\sigma_x\), \(E_-(\lambda) = -\sqrt{a^2+\lambda^2}\); energies in arbitrary units
Source: model knowledge. Standard treatment: Sakurai & Napolitano, Modern Quantum Mechanics, ch. 5; Bender & Orszag, Advanced Mathematical Methods for Scientists and Engineers, ch. 7–8.
