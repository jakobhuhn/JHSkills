What does a CNOT between two cat qubits do in phase space?

Two oscillators, each storing a qubit in a pair of coherent states. The gate rotates the target's phase space by half a turn, but only in the branch where the control is \(\ket{1}\). Watch both Wigner functions while it runs.

\(\ket{0} \approx \ket{\alpha}\), \(\ket{1} \approx \ket{-\alpha}\), \(\ket{\pm} \propto \ket{\alpha} \pm \ket{-\alpha}\), \(\alpha\) real; \(U(t) = \ket{0}\!\bra{0}_c \otimes 1 + \ket{1}\!\bra{1}_c \otimes \ee^{\ii\pi (t/T) a_t^\dagger a_t}\); ideal adiabatic gate
Source: model knowledge. J. Guillaud and M. Mirrahimi, Phys. Rev. X 9, 041053 (2019); S. Puri et al., Sci. Adv. 6, eaay5901 (2020). Widget: exact. Wigner functions of the reduced states in closed form, sampled on a 120 × 120 grid.
