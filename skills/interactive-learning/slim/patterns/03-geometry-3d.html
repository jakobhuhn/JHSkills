Why is every qubit state a point on a sphere?

Set the two angles of a pure qubit state and watch the arrow on the Bloch sphere, its two complex amplitudes and its measurement probabilities move together. Drag the sphere to rotate it.

\(\ket{\psi} = e^{i\gamma}\big[\cos\tfrac{\theta}{2}\ket{0} + e^{i\varphi}\sin\tfrac{\theta}{2}\ket{1}\big]\), \(\sigma_z\ket{0} = +\ket{0}\), \(\ket{\pm} = (\ket{0}\pm\ket{1})/\sqrt2\), \(\ket{\pm i} = (\ket{0}\pm i\ket{1})/\sqrt2\)
Source: model knowledge. Standard treatment: Nielsen & Chuang, Quantum Computation and Quantum Information, ch. 1 and ch. 4; Sakurai & Napolitano, Modern Quantum Mechanics, ch. 3.
