What does one half of an entangled pair know?

Two qubits in a pure state. Turn up the entangling angle and watch the coefficient matrix, the Schmidt spectrum and the state of each half. Then rotate each qubit on its own and see what does not move.

\(\ket\psi = \big(R_A(\alpha)\otimes R_B(\beta)\big)\big(\cos\theta\ket{00} + \sin\theta\ket{11}\big)\), \(R(\varphi) = \ee^{-\ii\varphi\sigma_y/2}\) (real); basis \(\ket{i_A j_B}\); \(\rho_A = \Tr_B\ket\psi\bra\psi = \Psi\Psi^{\mathsf T}\); entropy in bits
Source: model knowledge. Standard treatment: Nielsen & Chuang, Quantum Computation and Quantum Information, sections 2.4–2.5 (reduced density operator, Schmidt decomposition) and 11.3; Preskill, Lecture Notes for Physics 219, ch. 2 and 10.
