Condensed matter · Topological band theory

Why does the SSH chain have zero-energy states at its ends, but only for one dimerisation?

Two gapped phases with identical bulk bands. Turn the intracell hopping \(v\) and the intercell hopping \(w\), and watch the bands, the winding of \(\mathbf d(k)\), the finite chain spectrum and its end states move together.

Exact for the SSH model \(H=\sum_n (v\,c^\dagger_{n,A}c_{n,B}+w\,c^\dagger_{n+1,A}c_{n,B}+\text{h.c.})\), lattice constant 1; open-chain spectrum and states computed by exact diagonalisation
Source: model knowledge. Standard treatment: Asbóth, Oroszlány & Pályi, A Short Course on Topological Insulators, ch. 1; Bernevig & Hughes, Topological Insulators and Topological Superconductors.
